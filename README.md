Yes. Open the application, go to the Settings by clicking the gear icon, and click "Clear All History." You can also delete individual items by right-clicking them in the list.

When you copy something, it gets saved to your history on this computer, just like your normal clipboard. For extra privacy, you can use the "Pause History" feature in Settings, or manually delete any item at any time.

### What happens when I restart my computer?

Your clipboard history stays intact — it's saved permanently to your disk. nexus-clipboard will automatically restart in the background, and none of your saved items will be lost.

### Does it interfere with other applications?

No. nexus-clipboard runs quietly in the background and only activates when you open it. It works alongside all other programs without conflict.
